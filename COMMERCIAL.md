# Commercial licensing

SSHDeck is published under the
[PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE). That licence permits a lot and
costs nothing, but it does not permit commercial use. This page is how to get a
licence that does.

what scale. A quote and a written agreement follow; nothing on this page is
itself a contract.

## Do you need one?

You do **not** need a commercial licence for any purpose the PolyForm licence
already permits. Read the licence itself for the controlling text; in summary
it permits:

- personal use, hobby projects, private study, amateur pursuits;
- research, experiment and testing for public knowledge, with no anticipated
  commercial application;
- use by a charity, an educational institution, a public research organisation,
  a public safety or health organisation, an environmental protection
  organisation, or a government institution — regardless of how it is funded.

You **do** need a commercial licence for anything else. The common cases:

| What you want to do | Needs a licence |
| --- | --- |
| Run SSHDeck internally at a for-profit company, for its own staff | Yes |
| Offer SSHDeck, or anything built on it, as a paid or ad-supported service | Yes |
| Bundle SSHDeck into a product or appliance you sell | Yes |
| Use SSHDeck to deliver paid consulting, managed hosting or support | Yes |
| Use it at home, for your own servers | No |
| Use it at a university, hospital, charity or government body | No |
| Evaluate it at a company before deciding whether to buy | No — see below |

If your case is not obvious, ask. A short email is cheaper for both of us than
a wrong assumption.

## Evaluation

A for-profit organisation may run SSHDeck for **up to 30 days** to decide
whether to license it, on non-production systems, without paying and without
asking first. This paragraph is a permission granted by the licensor in
addition to the PolyForm terms; it ends when the 30 days do.

## What a commercial licence grants

The standard agreement grants, for the licensed organisation:

- the right to use, install and run SSHDeck for commercial purposes, including
  internally and in production;
- the right to modify it for the organisation's own use;
- use by that organisation's employees and contractors acting for it;
- the term agreed in the order, with updates released during that term.

It does **not** grant the right to redistribute SSHDeck, to sublicense it, to
offer it to third parties as a service, or to use the SSHDeck name or marks —
each of those is available, but is a separate conversation, not part of the
standard grant.

## Price

Quoted per organisation, on request. Tell me the intended use and the rough
number of people who will use it. Recognised nonprofits and public bodies do
not need a commercial licence at all, so there is nothing to quote.

## Support

The standard licence includes the software and its updates. It does not include
a support obligation or a service-level commitment; if you need either, say so
and it can be quoted separately. Security reports are handled for everybody
under [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), licensed or not.

## What stays the same either way

A commercial licence covers the licensor's own work. It does not and cannot
change the terms of the third-party components SSHDeck ships — the upstream MIT
code, the browser libraries, and Paramiko under the LGPL. Those keep their own
licences for everyone. [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md) lists them.

## Compliance

If you are using SSHDeck commercially without a licence, the practical path is
to say so and licence it. Coming into compliance is explicitly provided for by
the PolyForm terms, and a first approach is a conversation, not a demand.
